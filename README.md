# My work:
### [Slimbo Sam's Heist - Global Game Jam 2026 Entry](https://weegeeboii.itch.io/slimbo-sams-heist):
An autorunner platformer about a Slime named Sam robbing a bank, made in Unity. 
Created within a 48 hour time limit for [Global Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/university-of-staffordshire-site-ggj-2026-) alongside a small team involving two programmers - one of them being myself.

I worked as a programmer on this project - mainly developing features of the UI, menus, cutscenes and more - while also offering my assistance to another programmer on my team & communicating with artists and designers when discussing what assets we needed.

You can play our game [here](https://weegeeboii.itch.io/slimbo-sams-heist)!
### Star Car - University Assessment Project:
A top-down racing game, in which you must maneuver a vehicle through two unique laps of a racetrack on an island.
Made in Unity over the course of 9 weeks, while learning how to use the engine for the first time. The project was built off of an existing framework, provided by my lecturers at the beginning of the module.

During the module I was taught how to add moving obstacles, menus, collectables & more to my game - and also learned how to edit existing parts of the framework such as the tilemap, lighting & the sprites/animations of objects. 
I then used the knowledge I gained from this to add mechanics I came up with myself into the game, such as a dynamic map which changes when the player collides with triggers, hidden paths which reveal themselves when touched, gave character momentum, a gear shifting mechanic & more.
